# Check before you use anything in this folder

These were generated, not shot, and they are not uniformly safe. Several render
a fake UI with garbled text, and one carries **a different product's name**.
Putting any of those on the site would do the opposite of what the imagery is
there for.

Each file was opened and looked at on 2026-09-14. Verdicts:

## Safe — real photographs, nothing fake on screen

| File | Why it is fine |
|---|---|
| `step-01-attract.jpg` | A hand holding a phone showing real Instagram. Warm light, wooden table. Genuinely human. **In use** (optimised copy at `photos/channels-phone.jpg`). |
| `step-voiceqa-01.jpg` | A real desk phone in a real office, handwritten notes beside it, colleagues out of focus behind. Nothing on a screen to go wrong. Optimised copy at `photos/voice-desk.jpg`, not yet placed. |

`../editorial-office.jpg` is also safe — a real open-plan office, no legible
screens. **In use** (optimised copy at `photos/team-office.jpg`).

## Do not use — fake UI, garbled text, or wrong brand

| File | Problem |
|---|---|
| `step-02-engage.jpg` | Phone and laptop showing a chat branded **"LeadGen AI"** — not Fineguide. The dialogue is also garbled and the assistant's name changes between "Meye" and "Maya" mid-conversation. Shipping this puts another company's product name on our landing page. |
| `step-voiceqa-03.jpg` | Laptop lid reads **"MicSock Pie"** instead of MacBook Pro. Headings read "2YAITY EVALUATBN", "BATA SNRS"; "Usability" is spelled "Ueability". |
| `step-04-analyze.jpg` | The photo itself is convincing, but the dashboard on screen is invented and the data is malformed — "f0,300", "2,15l0", "Nl960", "San409" in a column of dollar values. It is also not our UI. |
| `ai-agents-01-connect.jpg` | Real WhatsApp / Instagram / Messenger windows, but every message is nonsense ("You hard vestine lovetine anerouct andlross helf"). Passable as a thumbnail, indefensible at full width. |
| `step-03-team.jpg` | Not a photograph at all — a flat purple vector illustration. Reads as clip art next to the real photography. |
| `../hero-bg.jpg` | Cyan particle-network abstract. The single most overused "AI company" stock image there is, and the cyan fights the indigo/violet brand. |

## The rule

If a screen is legible in the frame, read every word on it before shipping the
image. The failure mode here is not ugliness — it is a visitor zooming in and
finding that the product in our marketing photo is called something else.

Anything not listed above has not been reviewed. Open it and look before using it.
